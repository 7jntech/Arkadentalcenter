Did You Know?

Simple advice for a healthier, brighter smile

Kiwi: A Powerhouse for Your Smile

Gum Guard — High Vitamin C builds collagen to keep gums firm.
Natural Scrub — Fiber acts like a mini-toothbrush for your enamel.
Bacteria Fighter — Helps your mouth's natural defense system.
Stain Fighter — Enzymes help break down surface stains.

Brush for 2 Minutes, Twice a Day

Most people only brush for 45 seconds. The recommended 2 minutes removes up to 30% more plaque. Use a soft-bristled brush and replace it every 3 months for optimal cleaning power.

Flossing Cleans 40% of Your Tooth Surface

Your toothbrush cannot reach between teeth. Daily flossing prevents cavities, gum disease, and bad breath by removing plaque from areas your brush misses entirely.

Water Is Your Mouth's Best Friend

Drinking water after meals helps wash away food particles and acids that lead to decay. Fluoridated water also strengthens enamel and reduces cavity risk by up to 25%.

See Your Dentist Every 6 Months

Regular checkups catch problems early when they are easier and cheaper to treat. Professional cleanings remove tartar that brushing cannot, keeping your gums healthy and your smile bright.
